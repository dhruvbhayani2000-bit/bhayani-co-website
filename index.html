About the firm

A Chartered Accountancy practice in Ahmedabad founded by CA Dhruv Bhayani, combining traditional practice with enterprise-scale reconciliation and automation.

Our story

Big-company discipline for every client

Bhayani & Co. serves companies, LLPs, partnership firms, proprietors, salaried professionals and NRIs. We handle the full compliance cycle: books, returns, audits, notices and the registrations a growing business needs.

Our method comes from reconciliation work for large enterprises, where thousands of ledgers must tie out before period close. Every engagement runs on a checklist, every figure is traced to source, and every open item has an owner and a date.

Integrity

Work done under the ICAI Code of Ethics, with client data kept confidential.

Accuracy

Nothing is filed until it reconciles and has been reviewed by a Chartered Accountant.

Responsiveness

Clear timelines and quick replies, in Gujarati, Hindi or English.

    Common questions
    What do I need for my income tax return?

    PAN, Aadhaar, Form 16 or business books, bank statements, capital gains statements and proof of deductions. We send a personalised checklist after the first call.

    Do you work with clients outside Ahmedabad?

    Yes. Most compliance work runs online with secure document sharing. Audits that need physical verification are done on site anywhere in India.

    Can you handle a GST or income tax notice?

    Yes. We review the notice, reconcile the data, draft the reply and represent you in faceless or physical proceedings.

    How are fees decided?

    Fees depend on scope and volume. After the first meeting we share a written engagement letter with scope, timelines and fees before work starts.
