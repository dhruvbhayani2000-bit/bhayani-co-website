How to use the desk

  1. Start a company. Click Start my own company (or New company at the top), then type the company name and industry.
  2. Put in the figures. On Financials, type amounts in ₹ lakh, or click Upload financials → Download template → fill it in Excel → Choose file → Use these figures. The pill at the top must say Balance sheets tally.
  3. Pick the analysis year (normally the latest audited year) in the company card.
  4. Read the results. Overview for the health rings and things to look at, Ratios for all 48 ratios, DuPont for what drives ROE.
  5. Add peers. On Peers, add 5–10 listed companies (or upload the peer template) to see where the company ranks.
  6. Bank view. On Credit & CMA, enter covenant limits from the sanction letter, drawing-power margins and days past due; CMA Forms II–VI are ready below.
  7. Value the business. On Valuation, set growth, margins and WACC, choose weights, the marketability discount and the purpose.
  8. Take it away. Download Excel gives every sheet. Work saves to your account by itself.

All amounts are in ₹ lakh. Green = strong, amber = watch, red = weak. A full user manual (PowerPoint with screenshots and video clips) comes with this desk; keep it with your Finance Model files.
